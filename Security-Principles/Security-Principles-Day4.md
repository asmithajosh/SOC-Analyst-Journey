# Security Principles - Day 4

## CIA Triad

The foundation of information security.

### Confidentiality

Protecting information from unauthorized access.

Examples:

- Encryption
- Passwords
- Access controls

---

### Integrity

Ensuring data remains accurate and unchanged.

Examples:

- Hashing
- Checksums
- File integrity monitoring

---

### Availability

Ensuring systems and data remain accessible when needed.

Examples:

- Backups
- Redundancy
- Disaster recovery

---

## Authentication

Verifying a user's identity.

Examples:

- Passwords
- Biometrics
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

---

## Authorization

Determining what a user can access after authentication.

Example:

A user may log in successfully but still be restricted from accessing sensitive files.

---

## Principle of Least Privilege

Users should receive only the permissions necessary to perform their tasks.

Benefits:

- Reduces attack surface
- Limits damage from compromised accounts

---

## Key Takeaway

Strong security depends on protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability while enforcing proper authentication and authorization.
